Why Security Molding

We'll take care of it.

It's the sign on our wall and the reason customers stay for decades. Anyone can mold a part. Here's what actually makes the difference — and the proof behind every word.

01

We answer the phone.

In an industry where people don't pick up and don't return emails, we do — same day. Call us in an emergency and most of the time we already have a partial on the shelf; if you're truly stuck, we make it happen. Responsiveness isn't a slogan here, it's the whole job.

02

We stock for the bad week, not just the good one.

We keep months of hardware and partials on hand. That's why, when the supply chain broke in 2020–21 and other molders couldn't get parts, we kept running — and picked up the business they couldn't service.

03

We save your regrind — and your money.

Most shops throw the regrind away. We never have. Reusing it is a concrete savings we pass back to you, on every run, without making a fuss about it.

04

You get our best price the first time.

No gouging, no haggling games, no padding to leave room for a "discount." Dad's rule from day one was to always be fair — so the first number we quote is our best number.

05

We tell you the truth about fit.

We're a great shop for loose-to-moderate tolerance plastic parts and assemblies — and we're honest that we're not the place for medical or tight-tolerance work. If your part belongs elsewhere, we'll say so. That candor is exactly why customers trust us with everything else.

06

It's traceable, and it's documented.

Lot tracking, first-article approvals kept from day one, and photo work instructions in English and Spanish. Built on an ISO-grade system we never stopped running, so quality isn't a person — it's a process.

07

You'll know the people making your parts.

Family-run since 1972, now into the next generation, with a small cross-trained crew so someone always knows your job. Meet the shop →
