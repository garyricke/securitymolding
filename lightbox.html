Lightbox Cleanups

The part photos from the light box (Nov 24, 2026): each original and its cleaned version in one big before/after slider, with the exact AI instructions used and the hand fixes after. Use it as the recipe for the next batch.

1 · Shoot

iPhone 17 in the light box, or on the black mat or red backdrop. Convert the HEIC to a JPG about 2048 px tall.

2 · Clean up with AI

Send it to OpenAI with the base prompt below, the background line for that scene, and a short list of what’s specific to the part.

3 · Fix by hand

Check colour, red tone, holes and any text or tools; fix those with the tricks below, then upload to Cloudinary.

Base prompt

Every photo starts with this. For the black mat or the red backdrop, its last background sentence is swapped for that photo’s background line, and the photo’s extra instructions are added at the end.

This is a product photo of a single injection-molded plastic part, shot in a white light box. Retouch it into a flawless catalog photo of the SAME part.

Keep exactly the same: part geometry, every hole and its position and size, the molded texture/grain, color, camera angle, framing, lighting direction and soft shadow. Do not add, remove, move or reshape any feature. Do not add text or logos. Keep any intentional molded features (ribs, bosses, keys/notches that are clearly part of the design).

Remove only defects:
- flash: thin excess plastic or burrs on edges, hole edges and parting lines (make edges and hole edges clean and crisp)
- splay, silver streaks, flow lines, weld/knit lines, sink marks, gloss variation and scuffs on the surface
- ejector pin marks that are raised or sunken: make them perfectly flush with the surrounding surface, but keep the pin marks faintly visible as flat circular witness marks
- dust, lint, fingerprints, dirt and specks on the part and on the background
Make the background a clean, even, seamless white-to-light-grey sweep with no dirt or seams. Photorealistic, sharp, natural.

Hand-fix toolkit

  • Part too light: smooth Lab mean shift on the part pixels only, contrast clamped 0.85–1.1. The AI lightens parts along with the background.
  • Red too bright: scale R on saturated pixels to the original backdrop.
  • Magenta cast in red: hue lock: keep R, set G and B to the original’s G/R and B/R ratios.
  • Text, readouts and tools: the AI always redraws them. Warp the AI edit onto the original frame and paste the real pixels back through a mask.
  • Framing changed: align the AI edit to the original by feature matching, or by the part’s silhouette when the part is smooth.
  • Filled-in holes: paste the original hole back from the aligned original.
  • Leftover specks or fibers: paint out locally with a diffusion fill or masked blur.
