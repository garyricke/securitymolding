About cleaning up photos

A real shop is busy, so most of these photos benefit from a cleanup before they go on the site. We use an AI photo editor to make the shop look the way it does on a good day, and every result is checked by hand.

What we can do

  • Brighten and even out dim or mixed lighting
  • Remove clutter: trash, stray chairs, carts, cords, parked vehicles
  • Square up boxes, tidy benches, clean floors and walls
  • Straighten, crop and color-correct

What stays real

  • Your parts. We never change what a part looks like, its shape or its color.
  • Your machines, molds and the layout of the shop
  • Your people, exactly as they are. Anyone who appears on the site should be OK with it, so please check with them first.

Good to know

  • The editor sometimes invents small details, like an extra box or made-up writing on a label. Gary compares every cleanup with the original and redoes or drops any that don’t look right.
  • Close-ups of a part in someone’s hand, sharp and in good light, clean up best. Wide shots of busy areas change the most.
  • Nothing cleaned up goes on the site until you’ve seen it next to the original.

To request one: send the photo number and what it’s for, for example “7590 for Capabilities.” Gary will send back a before and after.
