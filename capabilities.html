Materials we run

The resins we know cold — because we run them every day.

Nylon

Our biggest material — including 33% glass-filled. The workhorse behind most window, door, and structural parts. Dried and run with care so humidity never shows up in your parts.

Styrene

Clean and fast — the material behind our screen and corner keys. It runs and runs, which is how we make millions a year without missing a beat.

PVC

Weep plugs and weep-hole covers by the million — the little parts that keep rain out of every window and door extrusion on the road.

Acetal

Tough, low-friction parts where you need durability and a clean finish.

Polycarbonate

Impact strength and clarity for the parts that have to take a hit.

Polypropylene

Light, chemical-resistant, and economical for high-volume components.

Working in another resin? Ask — odds are we've run something close.

Are we the right shop for your part?

We're a great fit for loose-to-moderate tolerance plastic parts and assemblies in the materials above. We're not a medical, aerospace, or tight-tolerance house, and we don't chase safety-critical automotive work. If your part belongs somewhere else, we'll tell you straight — that honesty is exactly why our customers trust us with everything else.

Quality & traceability

Built on an ISO-grade system — and kept that way.

We earned ISO 9001 certification years ago and never stopped running the system. It's how we keep our promises traceable.

Full lot traceability

Every job stored with material, lot number, dye, and the cards the team checks against — so we can trace any part back to the run that made it.

First-article approval

We sample, you approve, and the approved part is bagged and kept from day one — no "I didn't say that" years later.

Photo work instructions

Every assembly documented step-by-step with photos, in English and Spanish, so it's built right every time.

We save your regrind

We reuse regrind instead of throwing it away. Most shops toss it — we put that savings back in your pocket.
