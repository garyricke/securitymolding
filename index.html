Capabilities at a glance

Right-sized for the work that needs a real partner.

We're built for small-to-mid parts, honest tolerances, and runs from a few thousand to a few million. Not the biggest shop — the most responsive one.

Are we the right shop for your part?

We're a great fit for loose-to-moderate tolerance plastic parts and assemblies. We're not a medical or tight-tolerance house — and we'll tell you straight if your part belongs somewhere else. That honesty is why our customers trust us with everything else.
