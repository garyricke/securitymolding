Assemblies

We don't just mold the part.
We build the whole thing.

Rivets, springs, foam tape, wool pile, keepers — assembled, bagged, and ready to install. It's some of the hardest work to set up and the hardest to walk away from, which is exactly why our customers stay.

What we build

Complete, installable assemblies — not just a box of parts.

Egress latch handles

A handle that used to be aluminum, now molded in tougher, friendlier composite — built from three separate molds plus riveted hardware into one finished part.

RV door handles

Handle, catch, and keeper assembled and shipped together, ready to hook up on the line.

Spring & retainer builds

The fiddly little assemblies nobody else wants — springs, dogs, retainers, foam tape. We've got the jigs and the patience.

Kits & bagged sets

Parts counted, bagged, and kitted to your spec so they drop straight into your own build without sorting.

Hardware integration

Rivets, screws, nuts, washers, wool pile, foam — we stock it and build it in, so you get one finished assembly from one supplier.

Documented every step

Photo work instructions in English and Spanish for every assembly — so it's built the same, right, every time.

Why so few shops do this

The work that's hard to start is the work that's hard to lose.

Assemblies take real investment — the rivet machines, the jigs, the multiple molds behind a single part, and months of hardware sitting on the shelf. Most molders won't make that commitment. We made it years ago, and we keep it sharp.

That's why this work is sticky: once a part is set up and proven here, with the tooling paid for and the process documented, nobody wants to move it — and we make sure they never have a reason to.
