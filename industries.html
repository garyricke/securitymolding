Industries we serve

RV & Towables

Our roots are deeply embedded in the RV corridor. We operate on expanded schedules when regional demand peaks, positioning us as an early indicator of market fluctuations and a swift participant in recovery. We make window, door, and interior components for builders across the industry — and we ride the cycle right alongside you, with the responsiveness to ramp fast when the orders surge.

Window keys · weep plugs · latches · egress & door handles · interior components

Windows & Doors

The hardware that makes a window actually work. We mold radius and corner screen keys by the millions, weep plugs and covers in PVC that keep the rain out of every extrusion, plus latches, keepers, and the small structural parts that hold the whole assembly together.

Screen & corner keys · weep plugs & covers · latches · keepers · vent & lock parts

Mobile & Manufactured Homes

The durable molded components designed for our RV applications are equally suited for manufactured housing, engineered to withstand rigorous field conditions at high production volumes. This foundation of reliable, high-volume manufacturing remains one of our core competencies.

Window components · door hardware · weep covers · structural keys

Architectural & Commercial Glazing

Another sector of our business is manufacturing specialized window components for hotels and high-rise commercial structures. This collaboration is rooted in a decades-long partnership with respected customers whom we deeply value.

Commercial window components · high-rise & hospitality glazing parts

Consumer & Hardware Products

We specialize in manufacturing dependable consumer hardware components, including instrument wall mounts for guitars and violins, TV hangers, and specialized small parts. Product companies rely on our consistent production runs and our commitment to responsive, direct communication.

Instrument & wall mounts · TV hangers · spacers · consumer-product components

Transit & Specialty

We specialize in manufacturing small-to-mid-sized plastic components that larger manufacturers often overlook. From bus assembly parts and carbon monoxide detector housings to specialized niche applications, we provide the precision components that keep specialized industries running. If your project requires custom plastic molding for a unique or hard-to-find application, contact us to discuss your specifications.

Bus components · detector housings · specialty & low-volume parts
