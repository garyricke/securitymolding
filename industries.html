01 — Core

RV & Towables

We grew up with the RV belt. When Elkhart is busy, we run nights; when the market turns, we're the first to feel it and the first to come back. We make window, door, and interior components for builders across the industry — and we ride the cycle right alongside you, with the responsiveness to ramp fast when the orders surge.

Window keys · weep plugs · latches · egress & door handles · interior components

02 — Core

Windows & Doors

The hardware that makes a window actually work. We mold radius and corner screen keys by the million in styrene, weep plugs and covers in PVC that keep the rain out of every extrusion, plus latches, keepers, and the small structural parts that hold the whole assembly together.

Screen & corner keys · weep plugs & covers · latches · keepers · vent & lock parts

03 — Core

Mobile & Manufactured Homes

Many of the same window and door components that go on an RV go on a manufactured home — durable molded parts that have to last in the field, made in the volumes manufactured housing runs. It's steady, unglamorous work, and we're very good at it.

Window components · door hardware · weep covers · structural keys

Architectural & Commercial Glazing

We make window parts for hotels and high-rises — components that ride a different, steadier cycle than the RV market. It's a relationship that started decades ago with people we still enjoy working with, and it's exactly the kind of work we want more of.

Commercial window components · high-rise & hospitality glazing parts

Consumer & Hardware Products

The parts that found their way to us and stuck around: wall mounts that hang your guitar or violin, TV hangers, and other consumer hardware that needs a dependable molder who answers the phone. When a product company needs a reliable run of a small part, this is the work we love to pick up.

Instrument & wall mounts · TV hangers · spacers · consumer-product components

Transit & Specialty

Bus components, carbon-monoxide detector housings, and the niche parts other shops won't bother with. Nobody thinks about who makes the part on a bus — but somebody does, and often it's us. If it's a small-to-mid plastic part in a market we haven't named, ask anyway.

Bus components · detector housings · specialty & low-volume parts
