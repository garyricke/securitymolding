Our story

Making the parts nobody
thinks about since 1972.

Security Molding started with one machine and a simple promise — make the part right, treat people fair, and take care of it. Fifty-plus years later, that promise is heading into its next generation.

The road here

From one machine to 850 molds.

1972
It starts with one machine.

We trace our origins to a small shop located a block and a half away from where we operate today. What began on a single press producing specialized pins, bushings, and small plastic components has evolved into the cornerstone of our manufacturing expertise.

1978
Expanding our capabilities, preserving our roots.

The arrival of our first brand-new Arburg press marked a major milestone back in 1978, soon followed by our relocation to 255 Factory Road. This expanded facility houses increased press capacity and optimized infrastructure, positioning us for sustained growth while maintaining the dedicated, agile service of a specialized shop.

~2010
We build the system.

Months of dedicated effort culminated in our ISO 9001 certification. This milestone officially validates our rigorous quality management practices, including robust lot traceability, first-article approvals, precision calibration, and visual work instructions. We continue to follow this ISO-grade system — our commitment to maintaining these high operational standards has never wavered.

2020–21
Navigating the surge: how we reacted and pivoted during the pandemic.

During a period of unprecedented industry demand, we scaled operations to two shifts and doubled our workforce. While supply chain disruptions caused widespread hardware shortages across the sector, our proactive inventory management ensured uninterrupted production. This operational resilience allowed us to capture significant market share and forge lasting client partnerships that endure to this day.

Today
Into the next generation.

Under second-generation leadership, the business is guided by the founder's daughter, with his grandson gaining hands-on expertise across our machinery and client relationships. Supported by a highly skilled, cross-trained team, we remain dedicated to our foundational standards while strategically planning for future industry advancements.

A family business

Through decades of shifting markets, our core values have remained constant.

This philosophy is forged by our founder, who understood the critical balance between sustaining a livelihood and honoring a commitment to those who depend on our work. His guiding principle is simple: conduct business with unwavering fairness and integrity.

Today his daughter runs the business and his grandson is on the machines — learning what we make before he ever learns to sell it. As one of the crew put it, "he doesn't act like the owner's grandson." Around here, that's about the highest compliment there is.
