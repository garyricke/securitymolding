Deploy either email in EmailOctopus

  1. Campaigns → Create a campaign → choose “Import / paste HTML” (custom-coded), not the drag-and-drop builder.
  2. Click Copy HTML on the email you want and paste it into the HTML editor.
  3. Set the From name/email (a verified domain), the subject, and the preheader.
  4. Confirm the personalisation tag matches your list field: both emails use {{FirstName}} with a “there” fallback. Adjust if your field is named differently.
  5. Make sure the footer has your physical address and an unsubscribe link {{UnsubscribeURL}} — EmailOctopus can also append its own; don’t double up.
  6. Update the site link in the buttons if your live URL isn’t https://securitymolding.com.
  7. Send a test to yourself (check Gmail + Outlook + phone), then send to the “Existing customers” segment.

Both are built with table layout + inline styles so they render across email clients. Email 1 announces the site; Email 2 leans into the people and the engineering depth.
